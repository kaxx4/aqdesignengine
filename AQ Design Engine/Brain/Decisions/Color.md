# Color

- 7 accents, FREE per-slide rotation on social (do NOT category-lock; category-lock is web-only).
- Teal #0E7C86 is canon, the 7th accent.
- Ink text on lemon/sky/mint-bright; white on mint/tomato/grape/pink/teal/ink.
- Cream #F4EFE0 base, never white. Ink #0A0A0A, never #000. Dark = ink, never navy. No gradients.

## Standing bias (2026-08-03) — lead with green and blue

Free rotation stays legal, but this is what to reach for FIRST:

- **Primary carriers:** mint `#1B8A5A` and sky `#3DA9FC`. Teal `#0E7C86` and mint-bright
  `#00E5A0` extend the same family.
- **Constant highlight:** pink `#FF4D8C` on every slide — as the highlight swipe and at least one
  solid element. Pink never takes the field.
- Lemon `#FFC700` is the small third pop. Tomato and grape are occasional, not default.

Full reasoning: [[Green and blue LEAD the palette; pink is the constant highlight]].

## Outline colour is not fixed

"Ink outline" means ink **on a light field**. On an ink field the outline flips to cream — the
constant is the contrast, not the hue. See
[[Outline colour is a per-slide token, not a constant]] and the failure it came from,
[[Ink outline and hard shadow on an ink field (craft layer invisible)]].
