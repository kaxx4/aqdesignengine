# Green and blue LEAD the palette; pink is the constant highlight

**Session:** friendship_day carousel, 2026-08-02 → 08-03 (user directive, mid-build then reaffirmed)

## The ruling

Standing palette bias for AQ social work, superseding flat 7-accent rotation as the *default*
(free rotation remains legal — see [[Color]] — this sets what to reach for first):

> **Lead with green and blue.** Mint `#1B8A5A` and sky `#3DA9FC` are the two primary carriers.
> Teal `#0E7C86` and mint-bright `#00E5A0` extend the same family.
> **Pink `#FF4D8C` is the single constant highlight** — present on every slide, never as the field.

Lemon `#FFC700` remains available as a small third pop. Tomato and grape drop to occasional.

## Why this is stronger than rotation

Rotating a different hero accent per slide gives *variety* but no *identity* — an eight-slide set
reads as eight unrelated posters. A two-tier system gives both:

- **Tier 1, the variables:** blues sliding into greens with no hard border between them. These
  change slide to slide and carry the body of each composition.
- **Tier 2, the constant:** one warm high-frequency hue that never changes, never floods, and
  appears at the same *rate* everywhere. The eye learns it within two slides and then starts
  hunting it.

The hunt is the point. Consistency of the pink is what threads the set together, which is exactly
the job the per-hero-accent derivation in
[[Supporting palette now rotates WITH the hero accent, not independently of it (session 6)]] was
reaching for — this makes the through-line a *fixed* colour rather than a derived one.

## Applied shape (friendship_day)

- Panels + punch boxes rotate: mint → teal → sky → mint-bright → teal → sky.
- Pink is the `em()` highlight swipe under the key phrase on **every** slide.
- Pink is at least one solid element per slide (badge, bubble, figure, doodle cluster).
- Pink **never** takes a panel or a punch box. It punctuates; it does not shout.

## The ~30% rule still holds

Accents remain punctuation, not flooded fields ([[09 Brand Constants]]). Leading with green/blue
changes *which* accents, not *how much* accent.
