Before you come

  • Age: born on or after 1 January 2005. Every player in a team has to meet this.
  • ID: carry your school or college ID to every match.
  • Timing: report by the time on your card. The match window starts after it.
  • Rules: the umpire's and referee's decisions are final. No appeals. Full rules are on the rules page.
Same weekend

TerraThon Mini-Fete

3rd and 4th October, Turf XL, New Alipore. Mini-games, stalls, a photobooth and a lottery. Open to all, and all for charity.
