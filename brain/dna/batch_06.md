### d252704dc5
- WHAT: "Relationship Series — IT'S NOT YOU IT'S ME", a series title card for a talk/podcast episode about becoming the person you're called to be.
- ARCHETYPE: NEW: quadrant_patchwork (canvas split into hard-edged irregular color/pattern blocks — black field, cream field, checkerboard field — with type straddling the seams)
- READING AXIS: cream serif "Relationship Series" top-left → the blue sticker-label stack "IT'S NOT YOU / IT'S ME" mid-frame → down-right into the huge cropped black serif paragraph → out to the pink/red checkerboard bottom-left.
- HERO: the tilted blue sticker-label word stack, ~18% of canvas; but the black upper field + cropped serif block together carry equal weight.
- BASE FIELD: split — grainy near-black ink top half, warm cream bottom-right, saturated checkerboard bottom-left. Heavy paper/noise grain over everything.
- ACCENTS: 4 (sky blue, hot pink, red, lilac). Roles: blue = container fills behind label type; pink/red/lilac = large patterned FIELDS (checker + stripe), not punctuation. Deliberately un-AQ: accents flood.
- TYPE TREATMENT: sticker-label (blue boxes hugging each word), with serif-accent as the secondary voice
- EYE-CANDY MODE: contain (each phrase boxed) + juxtapose (spiky white/black starbursts pinned on seams)
- DENSITY: board-busy
- STEAL: box each WORD/phrase of a headline in its own tightly-hugging accent rectangle, each rotated ±2–4° at slightly different angles and horizontally offset so the boxes stagger rather than stack flush — the misregistration is the whole effect.
- REACHABLE: no (needs per-word tilted label boxes, hard-edged field partitioning, and procedural checkerboard/stripe pattern fills — none exist)
