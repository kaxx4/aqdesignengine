## 2. Campaign carousel template (Part 1)

Seven slides, each a different real photo (cover and closer are different moments):

1. **Cover.** Campaign name, its one-line description, one scoped number.
2. **The idea.** Why it exists, in the row's own words (`objective`, `short_summary`).
3. **On the ground.** Photo plus the row's `key_statistic`, verbatim.
4. **On the ground.** A second moment, a different place.
5. **Who ran it.** Volunteers per outing (the `volunteers` column), a student-run line, no names unless supplied.
6. **Where and when.** Span of dates and the places, from `location`.
7. **Next.** Where to follow it, or how to join.

Photo-poor campaigns get a type slide (the row's statistic as the hero), never a padded or reused frame.

## 6. Blocker: photos

The export holds roughly 400 site-hosted image links (`framerusercontent.com`) and 95 Drive folders
(`drive.google.com`). The cloud environment's network policy returns 403 for both hosts, so nothing
can be downloaded, looked at, or rendered. Allow `framerusercontent.com`, `drive.google.com`,
`drive.usercontent.google.com` and `lh3.googleusercontent.com`, or upload photos grouped by campaign.
