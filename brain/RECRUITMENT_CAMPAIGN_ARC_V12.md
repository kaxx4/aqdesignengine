# RECRUITMENT CAMPAIGN ARC (v12, 2026-11-13)

Idea: "we should totally..." Every group chat is full of plans that die there; AQ is where you get asked to help with one. Payoffs are invitations and what the experience is like, never stats. All chat lines are scripted, not real member messages. Script `scratchpad/gen_recruit_v12.py`, renders in `out/recruit_nov2026/v12/` (gitignored).

## The arc (four weeks, no dates)
1. Week 1, curiosity: informal carousel (B) on feed; standalone story posters (E), one a day; WhatsApp F3 in class groups.
2. Week 2, the full picture: formal carousel (A); narrative story (D) as one sequence; WhatsApp F2.
3. Week 3, the push: why-join stories (C) across the week; WhatsApp F1 sent member to friend.
4. Week 4: repost the best performer; C5 and the link story stay up; answer questions in DMs.
