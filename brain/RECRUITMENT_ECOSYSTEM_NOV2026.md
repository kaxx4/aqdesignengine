# RECRUITMENT ECOSYSTEM, "JUST A STUDENT" (2026-11-12)

Owner feedback that triggered this: the v2 designs were liked but "one-dimensional". A campaign is an ecosystem of messages that combine over one thing, it must cover everything AQ does, and it "cannot all just be events". v2 was five pieces, four of them on event or dance-floor photos, each carrying its own idea. This document replaces that approach with a system.

Sources, so every claim here can be traced: the live site (`/teams`, `/projects`, `/crftd`, `/schools`, `/classes`, `/opportunities`, `/blog`), `AquaTerra_Operating_Model.md` and the `AQ Dept-wise Goals & General Procedures Tracker` (Drive), the platform database (`welfare_projects`, 558 logged projects, 2021-06-19 to 2026-09-05, 205 distinct locations), and `AQ_FACTS.md`. Where a source disagrees with an older doc, the newer public source wins and the gap is listed in section 10.

---

## 1. THE ONE THING

**Real responsibility, from day one.** That is the site's own line (`/opportunities`), so it is already true and already ours. The campaign dramatises it with one device:

> **(just a student)** , a small yellow tab on every piece, followed by something no one expects a student to be doing.

The spine under every department is a fact nobody else in school has: **AQ takes zero donations.** Students earn the money (parties, school-fest stalls, sponsors, streetwear) and students spend it (drives, classes, trips, a tutor platform). So AQ is a small student-run economy, and every department sits somewhere on one loop:

```
EARN                                     SPEND
Events  (tickets, sponsors, stalls)  ->  Welfare drives (dogs, food, clothes, plantation)
Crftd   (streetwear, school stalls)  ->  Teaching (workshops, Sunday school, worksheets)
Collabs (partners, sponsors)         ->  Trips (Sundarbans, health camps)
                                         ShikshAQ, Classes (free, always)
KEEP IT RUNNING:  Social Media, Magazine and Blogs, HR, Schools Network
```

Every piece answers one question for one reader: **"which part of that loop could I be?"** The loop is the connective tissue. The device is the brand. The URL is the exit.

## 4. THE CONNECTORS (pieces that belong to every lane)

1. **The Loop.** "We throw parties. We sell T-shirts. The dogs eat." Earn, spend, zero donations. The single most centralising piece.
2. **The Doors.** All departments on one page, one form. Built from the website-page reference.
3. **The Day.** A timeline of one real day: 7am drive, noon vendor, 2am balloon count. Every lane appears once.
4. **The Path.** Community, then Team, then head of department. Told as a story of one real member, never as a ladder diagram.
5. **The Quiz.** "we're judging you in five questions." Results sort the reader into a lane and roast them gently.
6. **The Parent and the School.** One calm piece each, register 2, same facts.

## 5. WHAT KEEPS 60 PIECES LOOKING LIKE ONE CAMPAIGN

1. The **(just a student)** tab, top right, every piece.
2. A **lane chip** in the lane colour, top left of every lane piece, so the reader learns the colour code by osmosis.
3. **One real number per piece**, with its qualifier, taken from section 2.
4. The **reveal order**: situation, then "and yet", then proof, then ask. The ask is optional.
5. **Mechanisms are rotated, not owned.** The owner's five references (cast list, fill-in-the-blank, the note, the full-bleed hero, the website page) are formats. Any lane can wear any format, so the feed never reads as "that is the events post".
6. **Photo rule:** a lane piece uses that lane's own photo. An events photo on a welfare piece is a fail.
7. The exit is always **ngoaquaterra.com/recruitment**, in the footer or a pill.

## 7. FOUR WEEKS, NO DATES

No start date or deadline exists, so the weeks are relative. Exam fortnight: lean on WhatsApp and stories, thin the feed.

| Week | Question the reader asks | What runs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "wait, they do all of that?" | The Loop, The Doors, one teaser per lane |
| 2 | "which one is mine?" | a lane spotlight on most days, the Quiz |
| 3 | "is it real?" | programme editions and counts, member voices, The Day, Sundarbans |
| 4 | "fine. what do I do?" | The Path, the parent and school pieces, link stories, WhatsApp A |

## 8. CHANNELS

- **Instagram feed:** lane spotlights and connectors. One carousel per lane, built from real programme names (Tails and Treats, Sharbat Seva, Grow Green, Wear and Share, Book Basket) because the names are already funnier than anything we would invent.
- **Stories:** lane takeovers by actual members (first names only), polls per lane, the Quiz, link stories.
- **Reels:** one zero-AQ scene per lane. The reel rules stand: no AQ on screen, no CTA.
- **WhatsApp:** the two Community AQ groups (capped at 1,025 each), class groups, parent group. Forward-sized pieces and plain-text messages only.
- **Schools Network:** a notice-board PDF and a "bring AQ to yours" poster.
- **The funnel behind the link:** application form, a WhatsApp welcome, an interview, then the Community group, where a member can volunteer for a drive. Team selection is a separate application per department. The campaign must not promise a step the funnel does not have.

## 10. OWNER RULINGS (2026-11-12) AND WHAT IS STILL OPEN

Resolved by the owner:
1. **The eight departments:** events, welfare, Crftd, ShikshAQ, social media (marketing and social media are one department), blogs, collabs, HR.
2. **Crftd is the new name for ROOTS.** Write Crftd.
3. **Funnel:** every applicant gets an interview, and getting in is easy. **Do not talk about the process.** No step lists, no "how it works". The ask is just the URL.
4. **We recruit into Community AQ.** Once someone joins a department they are in Team AQ. Never explain the two layers.
5. **Photos with children:** full permission, for every frame in the library, and they are committed to git for future use.
6. **No Bengali, no adda-style lines** in the copy.
7. **Variation inside one campaign:** every piece uses a different mechanism (the owner's five references, then 17 more from the 74-style bank), held together by the campaign constants in `RECRUITMENT_BUILD_BRIEF_NOV2026.md`.
8. **Lane colours** follow the Social team's own code: events sky, welfare mint, Crftd tomato, ShikshAQ lemon, social media and blogs grape, collabs pink, HR teal.

Still open:
- **Magazine versus blogs.** The site's blog is Groundwork Diaries and the platform has a Digital Magazine team. One thing or two? (Pieces say "blogs".)
- **Real members.** Spotlight pieces need first names and permission. None have been supplied, so no person is named anywhere.
- **"One drive."** Is it still true that the first step is a single welfare drive? Pieces avoid saying it.
- **ShikshAQ.** Its own campaign brief bans "verified", zero commission, direct contact and the 11-days/48-hours research until the owner confirms them. Every piece now avoids all four. Which of them, if any, can be used?
- **Certificates and LORs** appear at most as one tiny line. Confirm that is the right weight.

## 12. V5: ONE MESSAGE, SAID DIRECTLY (2026-11-13)

Owner feedback after v4: "more direct, cleaner, more visually appealing stuff around a certain message." v2 to v4 gave every piece its own idea and skeleton, which read as variety but not as one campaign. v5 inverts it.

**The message:** *A student did a real thing, and this is it.* One sentence pattern, "A STUDENT [did X] THIS.", set in NeutralFace caps with the verb phrase on a department-colour highlight bar. Everything else on the page is cut.

**Every piece has exactly:** the logo, a department chip, the headline, one mono line with one real fact and its qualifier, and the URL pill. No doodles, no stickers, no second headline. Photos are full-bleed or in one thick-bordered frame, never collaged.

**Three layouts only** (script `scratchpad/gen_recruit_v5.py`): L1 full-bleed photo with a scrim and the headline in the lower third (or top, where the photo needs its bottom); L2 a framed photo on a department-colour ground; L3 cream ground with type and one real artifact (blog covers, the social team's own slides, a number, the board chips).
