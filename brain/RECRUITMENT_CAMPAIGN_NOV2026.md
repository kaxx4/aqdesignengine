## 0. READ THIS FIRST: three things that fight the brief

**1. AQ's own doctrine makes recruitment the rarest pillar (5-10%), and says reels must carry zero AQ mentions.** This campaign is a deliberate, time-boxed exception. It works only if we respect the reason the rule exists: people join personalities, not announcements. So the campaign leads with situations and jokes, and the ask arrives late and small. (Established fact: `aq-master-brief.md` as summarized in `AQ_FACTS.md` §12. Inference: a four-week burst will not damage the funnel if the proof-to-pitch ratio holds at roughly 80/20.)

**2. "Very direct" and "soft CTA" are compatible, but only one way.** Direct means specific: a named situation, a named role, a named day. It does not mean "JOIN AQ NOW!!", which is banned outright. The CTA ceiling stays "come once, decide after." Direct copy, soft exit.

**3. "All the benefits students get" is the riskiest line in the brief.** The truth ladder bans invented perks. Certificates, letters of recommendation, leadership titles, "college application boost": none of these appear in the fact bank. Section 4 splits benefits into **provable now** and **needs your confirmation**. If a benefit is not confirmed, it does not ship.

## 1. THE IDEA

### Insight (inference, validate with a few members)
Students are not short on ambition. They are short on a place to put it. School hands them a bell schedule and a report card, and everything interesting (running something, being trusted, being useful) is gated behind "when you're older." The funny part is how specific the stuck moments are. That specificity is the campaign.

### Core mechanic: **SITUATIONS**
Every piece opens with a situation a student is already in, written the way a friend would say it. AQ is the exit, and it arrives as a concrete thing you can do on a concrete day. The student never gets a description of what AQ is (Description Ban, invariant 15). They get the situation, then the proof.

### Three naming routes (pick one, I recommend A)

| Route | Line | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **A. SITUATIONSHIP** | "you're in a situation. we have a role for it." | Names the mechanic, works as a hashtag-free series title, and flexes into every department. Recommended. |
| B. NOT A CLUB | "it's not a club. clubs don't run 300-person fests." | Strong, but defines AQ by negation and drifts toward the banned "AQ is different" skeleton. |
| C. SCENE KYA HAI | "scene kya hai?" | Native Hinglish, very Gen Z, but narrows to Kolkata-Hinglish speakers and is harder to write benefits under. |

### One-line promise
**Whatever your situation is, there is a seat for it.** (Never "change the world". Never "make an impact".)

## 9. DESIGN DIRECTION (how this gets built in the engine)

- **Path:** Workflow C (`python design.py "<situation>"`), a bespoke script per poster drawn from `brain/STYLE_BANK.json`. Not the four registered archetypes. Each piece gets the looking gate on the rendered PNG.
- **Hero:** the situation line as display type, one italic Instrument Serif accent word per piece, max.
- **Colour:** use `core.accent_for(dept)` so a welfare piece is mint, events sky, content grape, etc. This lets the series read as a family while colour sorts the department.
- **Real assets only:** the corpus has 4 embedded photos (`food`, `edu`, `diwali`, `xmas`) and the carousel pipeline can pull real event photos from Drive. No stock, no fake UI, no fabricated QR or stats.
- **Don't flood:** accents ~30%, cream ground, craft layer on (outlines, hard shadows, one hero-shine).
- **Companion art piece:** per standing rule, I will invoke `/canvas-design` once alongside the first engine run.
- **Series skeleton:** consider a format module (like `dispatch.py` / `discodiwali.py`) once we have 3 approved posters. A 60-piece family decided one script at a time will drift.

## 9b. DESIGN INSPO, READ THROUGH AQ'S LANGUAGE (user references, 2026-11-12)

Five references were supplied (the "Most Beautiful Moment" moodboard, the lantern fill-in-the-blank, the "You are not alone" landscape, the CREATIE sky-and-pills hero, the Lexington course page). Their common thread is **real photography treated as one flat ink, with solid colour acting as punctuation on top**. That is already AQ's craft vocabulary (`tex.photo_ink`, duotone, hard-edged stickers, accent blocks), so none of it needs a new brand system. Each reference becomes one reusable template.

| # | Reference mechanism | AQ template | Used for | Engine pieces |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | B&W photo, saturated see-through colour windows over the people, each with a tiny label tab | **WINDOWS**: a real AQ event photo in mono, colour windows over the people, each tab names a department ("EVENTS", "WELFARE") | the "all the situations" carousel cover, department sorter | `tex.photo_ink`, duotone motif, `core.accent_for(dept)` |
| 2 | A sentence where the key words are replaced by tiny photo crops, source boxes marked on the photo below | **FILL IN THE SITUATION**: "sunday. nothing. again." with a photo crop standing in for the word that AQ answers | situation statics, story polls | inline crop thumbnails, real photos only |
| 3 | Posterised landscape, one frosted note card, one highlighted word chip ("alone.") | **THE NOTE**: one calm card on an ink-treated photo, one highlighted word | belonging posts, parent poster, "you don't know anyone there" | `photo_ink`, `shapes`, doodle stick figures |
| 4 | Huge bold headline with pill tags interlocking its lines, small squiggles | **PILL HEADLINE**: display type with department pills laid into the line gaps | feed statics, last-call poster | the built sticker-swarm motif (`CLAUDE.md` §12) |
| 5 | Serif headline with an italic last phrase, dithered photo tiles between flat colour blocks, numbered 01 to 04 | **BLOCKS AND DITHER**: one colour block per department, dithered member photos between them, numbered steps | department sorter results, "how to apply" story, school one-pager | `tex.photo_ink` dither, `shapes.pixel_art`, notched-slab motif |

### Where the references and AQ's hard rules disagree (precedence ladder, §2)
- **Serif-heavy headlines (refs 1 and 5):** AQ allows one Instrument Serif accent word per piece. Take the mechanism (serif carries the human voice) and express it as one italic accent word inside NeutralFace caps. Record the adaptation in each script's docstring.
- **Fake UI cards and available-for-work chips (ref 4):** real-assets-only rule. The pills stay, but they carry real department names, never invented UI.
- **Frosted translucent panel (ref 3):** low-alpha washes read as dirt (`wash_scan`). Build the note card solid, with an ink outline and hard shadow, on the cream or the photo.
- **White ground (refs 2, 4, 5):** AQ's ground is cream `#F4EFE0`, never white.
- **Stock landscapes:** none. Every photo is a real AQ frame (the 4 embedded in `core.PHOTOS` or the Drive carousel pipeline). Posterised cows in a field are replaced by a real welfare or event frame.

### Series rules so 60 pieces read as one family
1. **One photo treatment per piece, always real:** mono or duotone, never full colour behind type.
2. **One colour per department, from `accent_for(dept)`:** welfare mint, events sky, labs lemon, ops teal, content grape. Colour is how the viewer sorts themselves.
3. **One label system:** the small tab chip from ref 1, set in JetBrains Mono, used identically on every template.
4. **One italic accent word per piece, max.**
5. **Photo is the hero, colour is the punctuation (~30%).** If a piece floods with colour, it fails the looking gate.

The reference files are chat attachments, not on disk. If you want them scored or added to the style bank, drop the originals into `training_samples/` and I will run `compare.geometry` and judge them properly.
