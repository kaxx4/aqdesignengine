# RECRUITMENT COPY DECK, V13 (2026-11-13)

Owner direction: more depth, more structure, proper copywriting; better pictures with better taste; lead with education (ShikshAQ's free learning content, papers and tutors; teaching workshops), more welfare, how collabs and teaching are planned, TerraNotes for education and culture; Crftd minimised. No stats-as-brag, no pay, no named open seats, no process, no member names.

## The structure

The campaign tells one story in three layers.

1. **What AQ is, in one line:** a student-run organisation in Kolkata where most of the work is teaching, and everything else exists to make that possible.
2. **Why it matters to you:** you get handed something real (a classroom, a drive, a partner, a magazine issue) and the people around you are your age.
3. **The invitation:** start anywhere. Applications are open.

Weight across the campaign: education about 40% (teaching workshops, ShikshAQ), welfare and Sundarbans about 25%, collabs 10%, TerraNotes 10%, events, social and HR together about 15%. Crftd appears once, as a line.
