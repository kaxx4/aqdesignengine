### aq-master-brief.md — what it actually contains, section by section

- **Tone dimensions, scored 0-100** (richer than a simple 0-4 ladder): Formality 15, Humor 75,
  Chaos 65, Inspiration 40, Vulnerability 55. This is the real house-default register, more
  precise than `VOICE.md` §4's register ladder — use these five numbers as the actual target when
  writing general-audience/volunteer content, not just "register 3."
- **The voice in one sentence:** "your most interesting friend telling you about the insane thing
  their team just pulled off — not an organization asking you to care."
- **Personality archetype:** "the chaotic-good friend group that somehow gets things done."
- **7 real content pillars with real percentages** — see `IDEATION.md` §1, which now uses these
  verbatim instead of an invented 6-pillar system: Chaos Chronicles (25%), Impact Without
  Preaching (20%), Member Spotlights (20%), Skill Flex (15%), Local Identity (10%), Soft
  Recruitment (5-10%, **deliberately the rarest pillar**), Culture + Takes (5%).
- **8 numbered content rules, real and specific** (not generic sector advice):
  1. *The Real Person Test* — every carousel needs ≥1 real name/project/number/anecdote.
  2. *The Slide 1 Test* — the hook must work in isolation; cover slides 2-7 and check.
  3. *Proof Over Philosophy* — max 20% philosophy, min 80% proof. (This is the source of the
     "80% Proof / 20% Philosophy" ratio already in `IDEATION.md` §10 — confirmed, not just cited
     secondhand.)
  4. *The One-Time Rule* — **absolute, not a rolling window**: if a phrase/activity/description
     appeared in the previous carousel, it cannot appear in the next one. Stricter than
     `IDEATION.md` §4's "no exact pair repeats within 5 posts" — that rule is a reasonable
     approximation for pillar/post-type/lane combinations in general, but for carousel-level
     phrasing specifically, the real rule is a hard one-carousel cooldown.
  5. **The Description Ban — a real invariant `VOICE.md` §1 was missing entirely.** Never include
     a paragraph describing what AQ is ("AquaTerra is a student-run NGO where teenagers turn ideas
     into real projects" appeared in 6 consecutive carousels and is now permanently banned). "The
     content IS the explanation." Added to `VOICE.md` §1 as invariant 15.
  6. *No Judging the Audience* — never position members as superior to non-members; no "most
     people go home after school" framing.
  7. *The CTA Softness Rule* — "link in bio if you want in" is the ceiling; "Join AQ NOW!!" is
     banned outright, not just discouraged.
  8. *Never Guilt-Trip for Follows* — never equate not following with not caring about a cause.
     Direct, sharper validation of `VOICE.md` invariant 9.
- **A long, AQ-specific banned-phrase list** (not generic sector boilerplate like `VOICE.md` §9's
  original list — these are AQ's own accumulated failure patterns from real posts): "A student-run
  NGO where teenagers [turn/build/create] [ideas/free time] into [real projects/impact]",
  "Turning random ideas into real projects", "So, what's stopping you??", "Your schedule doesn't
  define your impact", any variant of "teenagers with ideas have always been a dangerous force for
  the status quo". Also a banned *structural* skeleton: generic-hook → "AQ is different" →
  abstract bullet list → description paragraph → soft-to-desperate CTA — replace with: specific
  hook → 1 slide of earned context → 2-3 slides of proof → friend-voiced CTA.
- **Reels have a completely separate, stricter rule set than carousels — a real gap in
  `VOICE.md` §5's channel table, now fixed:**
  1. Zero AQ mentions in the video itself — no on-screen text saying "AquaTerra," "register,"
     "join," or "apply."
  2. Zero CTAs in the video — no "link in bio," no "follow us."
  3. No preachy content, no inspirational messaging.
  4. AQ exists ONLY in the caption tag (@ngo.aquaterra) and the profile it's posted from.
  5. **The test:** if the reel could be posted by any random funny student account and still
     work, it passes.
  The real funnel this serves: HOOK (reels, zero AQ mention) → INTRIGUE (profile/grid) → DIVE
  (carousels/Stories, real proof) → PULL (a soft CTA only once someone has already consumed
  5-10 pieces of content). Reels are top-of-funnel only; the profile does the converting, not
  the reel.
- **Emotional hooks, ranked by real potency for this audience:** Curiosity Gap ("what ARE these
  people doing?") > Identity Aspiration ("I want to be part of something like that") > FOMO
  ("this is happening and I'm not part of it?") > Competence Signaling ("if I join, I get to do
  REAL things") > Belonging ("these are my people" — the retention hook, not the acquisition one).
  This is AQ's own real hook-psychology model; treat it as the primary lens for general-audience
  content, ahead of the borrowed engagement-goal-first axis in `IDEATION.md` §10 (that one's still
  useful as a secondary check, not a replacement).
- **A quiz + DM-automation content mechanism** — a real, detailed content format
  (`IDEATION.md` has no equivalent): a shareable Instagram quiz, delivered via DM automation,
  whose questions secretly map to AQ departments without the user knowing they're being sorted.
  Title must be a "mild threat or provocation" ("we're judging you in 5 questions"). Results roast
  lightly, never compliment corporately. The AQ connection stays nearly invisible until the very
  last line.
- **News-jacking** — a real content type: lead with a trending news story as the hook, pivot to
  AQ's own theme (e.g., "you don't need to be the best in the world, you just need to start"),
  AQ connection comes after the emotional setup.
- **Real posting rhythm**: minimum 3-4 feed posts/week + daily Stories, stretch to 5 (2 carousels,
  2 reels, 1 static/meme) — the specific day-by-day table is in the source file.
- **Real algorithm notes (2025-2026)**: 3-5 targeted hashtags, not 30; SEO keywords in caption/bio/
  alt text now matter more than hashtags; saves and shares outrank likes; first-hour engagement
  matters most.

  kolkata-born. slightly unhinged. 850+ members building things that matter."
