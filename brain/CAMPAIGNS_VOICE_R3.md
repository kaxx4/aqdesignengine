# CAMPAIGNS VOICE, R3 (owner interview, 2026-11-15)

Why this exists: the owner said the r1/r2 campaign copy had a "weird vibe". It sounded like an ad, tried too hard to be cool, went vague and poetic ("all of it is for someone else"), and didn't sound like AQ. This spec records the owner's answers from a plain-English interview, plus the full copy deck rewritten to match. It supersedes the r1/r2 copy and the lowercase r3 drafts.

## 1. The rules (owner answers)

| Question | Owner answer | What it means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| Who is talking | A friend in the group | Warm and specific, never a brand voice. |
| Clear vs friendly, when they clash | Headline clear, small line friendly | The big text states a fact. The small line under it can sound like a member. |
| Calibration sample | B: informative + friendly | See section 2. This is the reference register. |
| Text per slide | Depends on the slide | Mostly a headline plus one line. A few slides can carry two or three sentences. |
| Casing | Normal sentence case | "AquaTerra", "Kolkata", "ShikshAQ". No all-lowercase. |
| Humour | A small smile now and then | About one dry line per carousel. Never a joke per slide. |
| Pronoun | Mostly "we" | Show what we do. The reader decides. "You" appears rarely. |
| Who we are | "Our members" | Not "students", not "young people", not "a few hundred of us". |
| Emotion | Lightly, once in a while | One honest line per campaign at most. Photos carry the feeling. |
| Asking people to join | No ask on most posts | Only the Come in campaign and a few stories carry the link. |
| Numbers | Yes, plainly, where they help | A number sits inside a sentence as a fact. No giant brag numerals as the whole point. |
| Headlines | Mix of labels and sentences | Labels for simple slides ("Stray feeding rounds."), sentences where there's more to say. |
| Names of partners and places | Sometimes | Mostly on collab and teaching posts. |
| Slang | Some is fine | A word if it fits naturally. Never a whole line of it. |
| Adult reader should feel | Organised and trustworthy | Regular, registered, well-run. Facts over vibes. |

**Banned words:** heartwarming, smiles, give back / giving back, journey, passion, community (as a brochure word). Also avoid, from the earlier rulings: funding, pay, Pather Sathi, named open seats, the interview process, "make an impact", em dashes, Bengali or adda-style lines.

Note on "community": "Community AQ" is the internal name for the recruitment tier. It stays out of public copy under this ban unless the owner says otherwise.

## 2. The calibration sample (chosen)

1. **AquaTerra is a youth-run NGO in Kolkata.** About 1,400 of us, all between 14 and 19.
2. **Most of what we do is teach.** Workshops every week at shelters across the city. Chess, science, storytelling, whatever we can pull off.
3. **We also feed strays, plant trees and run drives.** The dogs on our routes know the sound of the biscuit packet.
4. **A registered NGO since 2021.** DARPAN-certified, in case anyone's parents ask.

The pattern: the headline is a plain, checkable fact. The small line adds one concrete detail, and once in a while that detail carries a small smile.

## 5. Design system, dialled down (owner interview, 2026-11-15)

| Question | Owner answer |
|---|---|
| What "dial down" means | One look across everything; less designed, more natural; quieter colours |
| Photo treatment | Stylised: grainy, slightly faded film |
| Text placement | Mix: on the photo over a dark fade, or on a plain band below it |
| Headline font | NeutralFace, all caps |
| Accents | Two, fixed: blue and green (sky `#3DA9FC` main, mint `#1B8A5A` support; teal fails small-type contrast on near-black) |
| Ground | Near-black with grain |
| Doodles | Rarely, one small one |
| Removed | Glass pills, frosted cards, hard shadows, thick outlines, slide counters and tabs |

Build: `scratchpad/gen_campaigns_r4.py`, output in `out/campaigns_r4/`. Two layouts only (`full`, `band`). Sky marks one key word per headline; mint is reserved for the occasional doodle. The logo sits in a plain cream pill with no border or shadow.

Gate note: `buried_text` reports every photo as buried under the dark-fade div. The fade is a `background-image` gradient with transparent stops, and `opaque()` counts any background-image as opaque. That is a false positive. The renders show the photos. Fixing it means teaching `opaque()` to read gradient alpha; it is logged here and not yet encoded.
