## The feedback loop (Haiku persona panel)
Personas: Riya, 15, Class X student (impatient, allergic to NGO ads); Mrs. Sen, 44, cautious parent (legitimacy, safety); Arjun, senior brand designer (hierarchy, legibility, photos, copy).

Round 1 findings and what was done:
- Same photos across campaigns (designer): photo ledger, each campaign now uses its own frames; a few unavoidable repeats remain where the library is thin (Sundarbans, chess).
- Text on busy photos (designer): stronger scrims on cinematic and impact slides; impact numbers sit on a dark gradient plate.
- Four campaigns looked alike (designer): ShikshAQ rebuilt as photo-top/lemon-panel; collabs got numbered steps.
- "aged 14 to 19" buried (student): moved to A1's line.
- Legitimacy (parent): new A7 "A registered NGO. DARPAN-certified with the Government of India."; registration line added to the collab cover.
- Copy: "strangers" cut; "Exam season? Start with the papers."; "Stuff no exam tests."; "Weeks of planning for one long day."; "Eight trips. The next one needs more hands."; "we'll show you how."; "nope. everyone starts somewhere."; impact lines dated "since 2021".
- Highlight icons didn't map to topics (designer): replaced with photo circles.
- Rejected on purpose: "tutors are checked by AquaTerra" and "an adult in every session" (parent) because nobody has confirmed them, and the ShikshAQ brief bans "verified"; a dated event with a phone number (student) because no real event details were supplied.

Round 2 scores: student 8/10, parent 6/10, designer 7/10. Fixes applied: large CTA chips on every closer ("APPLY ngoaquaterra.com/recruitment"), the Sundarbans closer has a photo again, "send us your first piece." for TerraNotes, the One Round cover caption in a lemon chip, What you'll learn reduced to two grounds, the One Round closer given a photo.

## Open for the owner
1. Supervision: the parent persona's biggest remaining gap. If true, a line like "every session has a team lead" would help; I have not claimed it.
2. A real first-event detail (date, place, how to show up) would move students from "maybe" to applying.
3. TerraNotes covers include member collages built on famous artworks (Vermeer, Van Gogh). They are AQ's own covers; confirm they are fine to post.
4. The One Sunday story is assembled from several real sessions, not one literal day.
