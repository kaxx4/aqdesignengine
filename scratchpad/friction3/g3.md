## Sec7 checks actually run, and how each was found

Run via `B.render(..., elements=, color_pairs=, page_bg=, expect_hero=True, text_pairs=,
containers=, collision_ignore=)`, i.e. through `render()` per the task's instruction, not a bare
`preflight()` call:

- `bounds_check`, `collision_check` (incl. `containers=` — see below), `quadrant_fill_check`
  (advisory) — via `elements=`.
- `css_var_check`, `img_src_check`, `same_as_bg_scan`, `wash_scan`, `double_rotation_scan` —
  auto whenever `html`/`page_bg` is passed to `render`/`preflight`.
- `invisible_color_check` — via `color_pairs=` (14 pairs: every fill against its TRUE backing
  surface, teal or lemon or cream-panel, not blanket page_bg).
- `text_contrast_check` — via `text_pairs=` (9 pairs, every text colour against its real
  surface, at its real px size and weight — this is opt-in and CLAUDE.md's own template doesn't
  demonstrate it at all, see below).
- `dominance_check` — via `expect_hero=True`.
- `reconcile.measure_dom` + `suppress_handled` + `reconcile_boxes` — auto in `_shoot`.
- `rotated_bbox` — used by hand for both rotated tag badges before declaring their bbox.
- `shapes.fit_font` / `fit_label` — used for both badge labels, with a real `fits` check
  (printed a WARN path if it ever returned False; it didn't).
- Manually, after the auto-gate: `preview.critique(png)` and `compare.geometry(png)` (the latter
  as a sanity read on the render, not a recreation score — Workflow C has no reference to score
  against). Did **not** run `antipattern_scan` (no rotated-overflow-anchored-bottom shape in this
  piece, so it would be pure noise per its own docstring) or `wash_scan`'s manual twin — it's
  already auto-included in `preflight`.

**Found only by reading source, not from CLAUDE.md's own text:**
1. **`containers=` on `collision_check`/`preflight`/`render`.** CLAUDE.md §7a's bullet on
   `collision_check` mentions only `ignore_pairs`; it never mentions `containers`, and the §6
   template's own `elements` list has no container/child relationship in it at all (its example
   is flat doodles on a page, not a panel with children). v1 declared a full-bleed `top_block`
   AND five children absolutely positioned inside it, and paid for the omission exactly as
   predicted by `collision_check`'s own docstring — five collisions, all `top_block` vs its own
   children. Fixed by finding `containers` in `layout.py`'s signature and reading its docstring,
   not from the manual.
2. **`text_contrast_check` / `text_pairs=`.** Documented in §7a as a one-line bullet
   ("HARD FAIL... every pair is one the author explicitly declared") but the §6 template has
   zero `text_pairs` in its example call to `preflight`, so there is no worked example of the
   tuple shape anywhere in the manual (`(label, text_color, surface_color, size_px, bold)`).
   I had to read `layout.text_contrast_check`'s docstring directly to get the arg order right.
3. **`shapes.sticker()` forces a SQUARE DOM footprint regardless of the silhouette's real
   aspect.** The docstring says so explicitly ("THE RENDERED BOX IS ALWAYS size x size"), but
   this is buried in `shapes.py`, not mentioned in CLAUDE.md at all — CLAUDE.md §9 just says
   "build every badge with `shapes.sticker()`". I only found it because a `tag(w=100,h=44)`
   (a 100:44 oblong) badge declared at size 170 showed up in `collision_check`'s output as a
   **192x192** box (`('top_block', 'tag_since2021', 192.00499884927808, 192.00499884927808)`) —
   170 grown further by `rotated_bbox` for the +-8deg tilt on top of the forced square. Two
   compounding surprises in one number; neither is guessable from the manual.
4. **`reconcile.measure_dom`'s selector reaches into flex children, not just
   `position:absolute` descendants.** CLAUDE.md §10's own catalog row for this fix ("`_SEL`
   measured only DIRECT children of `.p`... widened to every positioned descendant") implies
   the fix is scoped to *positioned* elements. In practice `_SEL = ".p [data-tag], .p
   [class*=measure], .p div, .p span, .p img, .p svg"` matches **every** div/span/svg in the
   tree regardless of `position`, so the plain (non-absolute) `<span>`s inside my flex-centered
   pill got measured and reported as `UNTRACKED` even though they are fully contained by the
   `giant_pill` box I *did* declare. Confirmed by reading `reconcile.py:132`, not from the manual.

## Was VOICE.md enough to write LinkedIn-register copy, or did I improvise?

Mostly enough, with three real gaps I had to close by judgment call:

1. **"ops" is not a lane.** VOICE.md's lane table (sec3) has welfare/food, climate, education,
   shikshaq, roots, animal welfare, health, clothes/kits, events — no lane for an aggregate
   cross-programme operations metric like "total volunteer turnouts logged." I improvised: no
   lane pull applied (pull=0, same as `clothes/kits`/`welfare`), audience=corporate/CSR
   (ceiling 1, because the content — a system that documents every turnout — is precisely the
   "AQ documents" pitch sec4 describes for that audience), intent=recap (sec6: "count it, then
   name who did it" -> ends on attribution). This produced good copy, but it's a genuine gap:
   VOICE.md gives no guidance for department-level/operational content that isn't about a single
   programme's activity.
2. **The truth-ladder trap needed defense-in-depth the ladder itself doesn't prescribe.** Sec2
   says "the qualifier travels with the number or the number does not ship," phrased as a
   single-instance rule. For a design with a giant hero numeral (the whole POINT of a number-hero
   mechanism is the number reads from across a room, divorced from its caption), I judged that
   one caption underneath is not enough insurance against the qualifier getting cropped/excerpted
   later (a LinkedIn preview crop, a re-share), so I put the qualifier in three places (inside
   the hero pill itself, and twice in the body copy's own prose: "3,756 turnouts... tracks
   visits, not unique people"). VOICE.md doesn't ask for this redundancy; I added it because the
   task called the trap out explicitly and the pill's whole job is to be legible standalone.
3. **LinkedIn's 40-120 word budget (sec5) is written for a post's caption, not stated to apply to
   on-image copy.** No image-only word budget exists anywhere in VOICE.md for any channel except
   feed poster's ("hero 3-7 words... body <=14 words"). I kept the on-image body to ~2 short
   sentences (38 words) plus a 7-word attribution line, closer to feed-poster proportions than
   the stated LinkedIn 40-120, because a poster with 120 words of body copy at a legible size
   does not fit a 1200x1200 canvas. This is a real gap: sec5's table conflates "the channel" with
   "the post's caption text," and a LinkedIn IMAGE (this deliverable) is a different object from
   a LinkedIn TEXT POST — VOICE.md has no row for the former.

Sec9's do-not-say list, sec1's invariants (no em dash, no emoji, lowercase body/lowercase-except-
LinkedIn, soft close) and sec7's precedence cascade (audience caps -> intent shapes -> lane fills
-> channel cuts) were all directly usable with zero ambiguity — those parts of VOICE.md are the
strongest-written part of the whole system.

## Manual (CLAUDE.md) gaps found

- **§2's Workflow C description says design.py "prints a BRIEF; it does not render" and gives no
  worked example of turning a drawn STYLE's `recipe` text into element coordinates for a
  DIFFERENT aspect ratio than the reference.** The RE-PROPORTION warning it prints is good and
  actually the most useful single line of guidance in the whole run — but it's generated by
  `design.py`, not written in CLAUDE.md, so a reader who doesn't run the command never sees the
  warning that a landscape reference re-solved for a square canvas needs its own layout decisions
  (I had to decide top-block-height-as-fraction myself; nothing in §5/§6 addresses re-proportioning
  since Workflow B recreations by definition keep the reference's own aspect).
- **§6's template never demonstrates `text_pairs=`, `containers=`, or `rotated_bbox` together in
  one call**, despite §7a documenting all three as real, load-bearing arguments. A reader who
  copies the template literally (as instructed: "Copy this into the scratchpad") will ship a
  build that a) never declares `text_pairs` (so `text_contrast_check` never runs even though it's
  a HARD FAIL check) and b) collides every child against its own parent panel the first time the
  build uses a full-bleed container div with children (extremely common — the §6 template itself
  doesn't have one, so this is invisible in the template's own self-test).
- **§9's "build every badge with `shapes.sticker()`" doesn't warn about the square-footprint
  behaviour**, which changes what "the badge's bbox" means for every downstream gate
  (`collision_check`, `bounds_check`, `rotated_bbox`) — you have to declare a bigger, squarer box
  than the badge visually occupies, or every gate is checking against a box smaller than what's
  actually drawn. This is exactly the class of bug §10's catalog exists to prevent, and it isn't
  in the catalog.
- **§7c's `compare.geometry`/`compare.crop` section is written entirely for Workflow B**
  ("measure the reference BEFORE describing it"). For Workflow C there is no reference to hold
  the render to (no "acceptance checklist" from step 1 of Workflow B — Workflow C's own
  checklist in §2 says "measure the chosen style's reference... build to its proportions" but
  never says what to do when the STYLE's own aspect ratio doesn't match the canvas being built,
  which `design.py` flags on essentially every non-matching-canvas draw).

## API surprises

- **Confirmed, not a surprise but worth reinforcing**: `dd.stamp()` and `shapes.fit_font()` /
  `shapes.label()` behaved exactly as documented — no silent kwarg drops, no unit-trap hits (I
  used `fit_font`'s returned BOX units directly into `label(size=...)`, never guessed a px value).
- **`same_as_bg_scan` (auto in `render()`) cries wolf on any element whose fill is literally the
  page's cream, regardless of what surface it actually sits on.** My cream logo-pill and both
  cream tag badges sit on the TEAL top block, not on the page — but the scan only knows "declared
  background-color == resolved page_bg," so it printed
  `⚠ FILL SAME AS PAGE BG (element invisible): [('#F4EFE0', '#f4efe0', 0.0)]` on every single
  render, v1 and v2 alike, for elements that were never actually invisible (verified by eye both
  times — they sit clearly on teal). Its own docstring says it deliberately skips full-bleed
  backdrops via an `inset:0`/100% heuristic; it has no equivalent heuristic for "this fill is
  cream but its surface is not the page." There's no parameter to exempt a label. It's advisory
  (a `print`, not part of `preflight`'s `clean`), so it never blocked anything — but it is a gate
  crying wolf on every render of this piece, exactly the failure mode CLAUDE.md §7's own
  `under_filled_quadrants` noise-suppression logic says is worse than no check.
- **`reconcile_boxes`'s `UNTRACKED` line fires on text nested inside a flex container even when
  a parent box fully covering it WAS declared** — `⚠ (advisory) UNTRACKED span at (431,287)
  338x137 — no declared bbox, so no gate ever considered it` refers to the `<span>` holding
  "3,756" + "VOLUNTEER TURNOUTS" inside `giant_pill`, which I did declare
  (`("giant_pill", 240, 250, 720, 240)` fully contains `(431,287,338,137)`). The check has no
  concept of "contained by a declared ancestor," so it's a false "untracked," not a real gap —
  but the wording ("no gate ever considered it") overstates the problem, since collision/bounds
  checks on the parent box already cover this region.
- **`fit_font`'s `measured_at` argument must match a REAL `measure_text` call at that exact px
  size** — this worked fine once I did it right, but the API gives no runtime check that
  `measured_w` and `measured_at` actually came from the same font/weight/transform as the label
  you're about to draw. Passing a mismatched pair (e.g. measuring at weight 700 and rendering the
  label at weight 800) would silently produce a wrong-but-plausible box size with no error. Not
  hit in this build (I kept weight=800 consistent both places), but it's a footgun with no gate
  behind it.
- **`render()`'s inferred `page_bg`** (the `_bg = re.search(r'\.p\{[^}]*?background:...')` fallback)
  worked correctly and matched what I passed explicitly — no surprise there, just confirming the
  auto-inference path is sound.

## Iteration log

- **v1** (~most of the build time: reading `shapes.py`/`doodles.py`/`layout.py`/`core.py` source
  to get badge-fitting, accent, and contrast APIs right cold, then one script write + one render).
  Render itself was clean-looking by eye on first try (number-hero + shoulder badges + paper
  panel all present and legible), but the gate found 3 real things the eye also confirmed on a
  crop-in: (a) 5 collisions, all `top_block` vs. its own children — fixed by `containers=`;
  (b) the hero numeral's line-height-0.78 descender (the comma) visually touching "VOLUNTEER"
  underneath it, confirmed by cropping the pill region; (c) a genuine dead zone in the paper
  panel between the body copy and the footer (~300px of empty cream), which is exactly the
  "dead half the reference fills" class in CLAUDE.md's own bug catalog (row: sample 15/25/30/31/
  39, guard `preview.critique` dead_quadrant/sparse).
- **v2** (fast — maybe 15% of v1's time): fixed all three above. For (c), rather than padding
  with a filler doodle (CLAUDE.md's own stated last resort), I added the RECAP intent's own
  missing half per VOICE.md sec6 ("count it, then name who did it" — attribution) as a real
  second sentence, which is content-correct (recap intent should have ended on attribution
  anyway; v1 simply never wrote it) and also solved the density problem. Result: `preflight`
  printed `CLEAN` outright, `text_contrast_check` passed on all 9 pairs, and the crop-in review
  confirmed both the pill-gap and dead-zone fixes.
- No 3rd iteration was needed — the looking gate's only two remaining prints after v2 are the two
  documented false-positives above, neither a real defect.
